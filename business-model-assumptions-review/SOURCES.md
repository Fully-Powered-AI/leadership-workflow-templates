# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The starting idea, laying a business model out in blocks and marking for each how much real evidence stands behind it, comes from the business model canvas package in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0), in its innovation/skills/canvas folder; its bar for calling something proven, that customers have paid or kept using it, shaped our evidence rule. Fully Powered AI's own: six blocks, marking each claim instead of each block, five kinds of evidence of which only three count, dated evidence with an age limit, a two-scale score with a fixed tie order, a ladder of cheaper-to-dearer tests with a default rung for each block, pass and fail lines left for the sponsor to set before any test, a blank choice line for the sponsor, and the refusal to project or value. No third-party text is reused.
