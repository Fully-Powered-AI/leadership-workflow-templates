# Executive situation brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Company K is an invented 45-person business that makes stainless-steel shelving and benching for commercial kitchens. Partner D is an invented distributor that sells Company K's products in the North region. Every company, person, record and figure here is invented. Noor, Company K's CEO, has a video call with Partner D's managing director and sales director on Thursday 5 November 2026. Tomas, her chief of staff, runs the workflow template on Tuesday 3 November. First come the inputs he supplied, in full, then the brief the template should produce, written out by hand to follow its rules.

## The inputs

### The request

```
Today is Tuesday 3 November 2026. Prepared by Tomas (chief of staff) for
Noor (CEO).
Subject: Partner D, North renewal and the East region.
Conversation: Thursday 5 November 2026, 10:00, 60 minutes, video call.
With: Partner D's managing director and sales director.
From us: Noor (CEO) and Ana (sales director).
Question to settle, in Noor's words: "Do we offer Partner D anything on
East before the 2027 plan is approved on 1 December, and if so, what do
we ask in return?"
Noor also wants this choice listed: renew North only, on the current terms.
Noor also asked: "And tell me how to handle her; she's tough in these
meetings."
Settings: the defaults.
Approved records: R1 to R6 and the decision log export below. Also
attached: East price sheet v2.
```

### R1. Partner D: partnership terms summary

```
Prepared by Priya (finance lead), Monday 12 January 2026. APPROVED.
- Territory: North region. Partner D is our only distributor there.
- Term: 1 January 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- 2026 target: 1,200 units.
- Discount: 18% off list on all orders.
- Renewal: by agreement of both sides. Either side may give notice that it
  will not renew by 31 December 2026.
```

### R2. Quarterly review with Partner D: notes

```
Tuesday 14 July 2026. Notes by Ana (sales director).
Present: Partner D's managing director and sales director; Noor, Ana.
- First-half units: 480 against 600 planned.
- Partner D asked to be our distributor in the East region as well, "so
  the numbers work for us".
- Noor: "We'll look at East once North is back on target."
- Agreed: Ana to send Partner D a co-marketing proposal by 31 July.
- Partner D's MD is back from leave for a family matter; asked us to keep
  that between us.
```

### R3. Email from Partner D's managing director to Noor

```
Friday 2 October 2026. Subject: 2027

Noor, thanks for the co-marketing support this autumn. Our Q3 orders with
you were 310 units.

For 2027 we can commit to 1,500 units a year across North and East if we
have both regions exclusively from April 2027. Without East, we will plan
for about 1,000 units in North next year.

We would need a further 4% discount on East orders to cover the cost of
opening there.

As we read the current terms, if we reach 1,000 units this year the North
exclusivity rolls over anyway.

We'll send you our 2027 North forecast by the end of October.
```
