Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the situation-brief method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes: preparing one brief either before a meeting or before a decision; opening it with a single sentence on where things stand; giving the background, including the decisions taken so far; stating on its own line the single matter to settle; laying out the options; and recording where each person involved stands. The source's profiles of individuals and their manner, its advice on how the reader should proceed, its list of risks, its local memory file and its other brief types are not used. Fully Powered AI added: building the brief only from records the executive approves, with drafts set aside and every line naming its record; the decision log export with stated column headings and a stop when one does not match; each promise either side made, with whether it was kept; positions given only as each side stated them, quoted or marked as a note, with old statements marked; both versions shown wherever two records disagree; choices limited to those someone actually raised, unranked, with making no commitment always among them and a flag where agreeing on the day would go against a decision in force; the dates that matter, counted from the conversation; questions about what the terms mean listed for the company's advisers with no view given; personal matters left out and counted by kind; requests the workflow template declines listed openly; and the rule that a person decides and nothing is sent. No third-party text is reused.
