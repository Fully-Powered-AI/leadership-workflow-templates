# Weekly owner business brief

Each Monday the owner gets about one page built from the exports they supply: cash, last week's sales against the week before, pipeline movement, the week's commitments and a watch list, with one item to look at first. Every figure names its source, and every missing export is named.

*Workflow template · Leadership · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/weekly-owner-business-brief/)*

## The outcome

The owner starts the week knowing where the business stands before the first call comes in. Cash, sales, the pipeline and the diary sit on one page in the same order every week, each figure beside the week before it, so a change is easy to see.

The page names where each figure came from and the date of that export. If an export was not supplied, its section says so and shows no figure; nothing is estimated to fill the space. A blank in an export is treated as unknown, never as zero.

One item is put first. It is chosen by a fixed rule that the page states, so the owner can see why it is there and overrule it. The owner decides what comes first this week and what to pass to someone else. The page makes no calls, sends no messages and changes no records.

## Who starts the work, and when

The owner or managing director starts it on Monday morning, or an assistant or office manager runs it for them over the weekend. It needs the exports the business already produces: the bank or ledger balances, a sales list, this week's and last week's pipeline lists, the calendar and, if wanted, a list of unpaid invoices. A few minutes of the owner's typed notes can go in too.

## What they supply

- **Settings.** The run date, the accounts to hold back from available cash, a cash floor if there is one, and the limits for a fall in sales, a quiet deal and an overdue invoice.
- **Cash balances,** on two dates a week apart.
- **Sales,** by day and channel, covering the last two weeks.
- **The pipeline,** as exported this week and last week.
- **The calendar,** for the week ahead, with any payments that fall due.
- **Unpaid invoices,** optional, to name the ones well past due.
- **The owner's notes,** optional: anything already on their mind.

## What comes out

About one printed page in a fixed order: the item to look at first, cash, sales, pipeline movement, the week's commitments, a watch list, questions for the owner, and a sources line naming every file used and every one missing. If it runs long, the wording is shortened; no section, source or question is dropped to fit. Pipeline movement ends with a line that proves it: last week's open value, less what was won, lost or changed, plus what is new, equals this week's. If that does not add up, nothing is drafted until the cause is found.

## What has to change for your company

Set your own limits: the cash floor, the fall in sales that counts, how many days a deal may sit untouched and how late an invoice has to be before it is named. List your pipeline stages in order. Decide which accounts count as available cash and which are held back, such as a tax reserve. Check that your exports use the column headers the template expects, or change the headers it expects once, in one place. Decide who runs it, on which day, and where the owner reads it. It is written to run in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from files you supply, and needs no connection to your systems.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
