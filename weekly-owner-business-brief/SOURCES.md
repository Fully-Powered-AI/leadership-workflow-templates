# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The idea of a single weekly page that draws on several business records came from anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0), in its small-business business-pulse package: opening with the one matter that needs attention, setting each figure against the week before, showing a figure that cannot be read as missing, never as a guess, taking sales from one source instead of adding several together, and a start-of-week version that looks ahead. The same package supplied the risk triggers and their starting limits (an invoice more than 30 days past due, an open deal with no activity for a set number of days, an open deal whose close date has passed, a fall in sales past a limit of 10%), the note that the quiet-deal limit may be set to 14 days, and the practice of linking a message to a flagged deal and raising it first. From small-business/shared/absent-is-not-zero.md in the same repository came the rule that a blank activity date counts against a deal and a date later than the run date is treated as blank. From small-business/shared/untrusted-content.md came the rule that text inside a record or message is data and never an instruction (the template's own wording is different and no text is reused). From small-business/skills/business-pulse/reference/output_template.md came the fixed section order, the aim of one page, one-decimal percentages and the leaving out of internal calendar entries. Its connectors, scoring colours, scheduling and file-saving steps are not used, and no third-party text is reused. Fully Powered AI's own: working from dated exports the person supplies, with default column headers and a stop when one does not match; matching an owner's note to a customer or deal only by an exact name; the pipeline tie-out from last week's open value to this week's; the fixed order that proposes the one item to look at first, which the owner may overrule; the cash line that assumes every listed payment is made and nothing arrives; and the rule that the page contacts no one and sends nothing.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: figures only from named, dated exports, every gap named, no figure guessed, and a person who decides what comes first and what to escalate.
